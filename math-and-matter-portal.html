Daily workflow — how to get the most out of every lesson
1
Watch the video
Take notes while watching. Pause and rewind freely. Don't just passively listen.
2
Attempt the practice questions
Close your notes and try the questions on your own first. That's when real learning happens.
3
Mark using the memo
Go through carefully. Find exactly where marks were lost and why. Write down those mistakes.
4
Tick off in your dashboard
Only tick "Understood" if you'd get it right in an exam tomorrow. Be honest with yourself.
